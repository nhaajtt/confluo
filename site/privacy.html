Nothing leaves your computer

The app

Confluo has no account, no advertising and no telemetry. It contacts only the servers you ask it to download from. Its list of downloads and its settings stay in your user profile on your PC. Cookies you choose to pass along for a download are encrypted with Windows before they are saved and are sent only to the site they belong to.

The browser extension

The extension collects nothing and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It talks to exactly one address, 127.0.0.1, which is the Confluo app on your own computer. When you start a download, it passes the link, the suggested file name and the page it came from to the app. If, and only if, you turn on “send cookies”, it also passes that site's cookies to the app so the download is allowed. The pairing code you paste is kept in the browser's local storage and is never transmitted: each side only proves that it knows it.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, fonts or images. Like any website, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes the address your request comes from in order to deliver the page.
